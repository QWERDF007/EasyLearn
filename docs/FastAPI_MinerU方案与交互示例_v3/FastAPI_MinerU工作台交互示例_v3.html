How Do Vision Transformers Work?

Alexey Dosovitskiy, Lucas Beyer, Alexander Kolesnikov, et al.

While the Transformer architecture has become the de-facto standard for natural language processing tasks, its applications to computer vision remain limited. In vision, attention is either applied in conjunction with convolutional networks, or used to replace certain components of convolutions.

1. Introduction & Method

To handle 2D images, we reshape the image into a sequence of flattened 2D patches. The Vision Transformer (ViT) feeds these flattened patches into a standard Transformer encoder with position embeddings:

z0 = [xclass; xp1E; xp2E; ...; xpNE] + Epos     (1)

The Transformer encoder consists of alternating layers of Multi-Head Self-Attention (MSA) and Multi-Layer Perceptron (MLP) blocks. Layernorm (LN) is applied before every block, and residual connections after every block:

Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax( Q KT / √dk ) V     (2)
尽管 Transformer 架构已成为自然语言处理任务的事实标准，但其在计算机视觉领域的应用仍然相对有限。在视觉领域中，注意力机制通常与卷积神经网络结合使用，或者用于替换卷积网络的某些组件。
